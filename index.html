FAQ

What's included?
The Desert Southwest region (13 parks, including Grand Canyon, Zion and Arches) comes with every quiz. The All 63 Parks in-app purchase unlocks the other five regions, Which Came First?, Review Mistakes, the All 63 Challenge and the full Field Guide. It works with Family Sharing. There are no ads.

What are the quizzes?
Picture the Park (name the park from a photo), Pin It (find the park among four pins on the map), Name the Spot (which park is at the pin?), Which State?, and Clue Cards (guess the park from a ranger's clue). With All 63 Parks you also get Which Came First?, Review Mistakes and the All 63 Challenge.

How do I earn a patch?
Each park's patch has three parts: name it from a photo, find it on the map, and solve its clue. Get all three right and the patch is yours. Your patches fill in the Patch Map, and your rank goes up as you collect more.

I bought All 63 Parks, but it's locked on my new device.
Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and tap Restore Purchase while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Where do the photos come from?
The park photos are in the public domain or CC0, mostly works of the U.S. National Park Service and other U.S. government agencies, found through Wikimedia Commons. Credits are listed in the app under Settings > Photo Credits.

Is this an official National Park Service app?
No. Ranger Ready is an independent learning app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Park Service.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

Ranger Ready does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your quiz progress and patches are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Ranger Ready works offline and does not connect to our servers. The Support and Privacy Policy links in the app open this page in your browser.

Because we collect no data, the app is suitable for children. If you have questions about this policy, email tajimaru1209@gmail.com.
